Case Studies

Selected examples demonstrating how Cigniti Technology Inc supports enterprises with complex, regulated, and mission-critical digital initiatives.

Digital Assurance for a Regulated Financial Services Platform

Industry: Financial Services | Engagement Type: Managed Assurance

A large financial services organization required independent digital assurance across a multi-platform environment subject to strict regulatory and security controls.

Challenge

Frequent releases, legacy dependencies, and regulatory oversight created elevated delivery and compliance risk.

Approach

Implemented AI-led test automation, risk-based assurance, and governance-aligned reporting integrated with release cycles.

Outcome

Improved release confidence, reduced defect leakage, and enhanced audit readiness without impacting delivery velocity.

Quality Engineering for a Healthcare Data Platform

Industry: Healthcare | Engagement Type: Project-Based

A healthcare organization modernizing its data platform required assurance that clinical and operational data integrity would be preserved throughout transformation.

Challenge

High sensitivity of data, complex integrations, and regulatory compliance requirements limited tolerance for defects.

Approach

Established quality engineering pipelines, data validation frameworks, and security-focused testing practices.

Outcome

Supported platform launch with validated data integrity, stable integrations, and compliance-aligned delivery.

AI-Assisted Assurance for an Enterprise SaaS Provider

Industry: Technology | Engagement Type: Strategic Partnership

A global SaaS provider required scalable quality assurance as product complexity and release frequency increased.

Challenge

Rapid feature expansion increased regression risk while maintaining customer SLAs.

Approach

Deployed AI-assisted test prioritization, automation, and continuous quality analytics.

Outcome

Reduced regression cycles, improved release predictability, and supported long-term platform scalability.
